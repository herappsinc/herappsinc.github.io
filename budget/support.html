Her Budget Support

Help with logging, accounts, Premium and deleting your account

Need help? Email herappsinc@gmail.com and we'll usually reply within 2 working days. It helps if you tell us your phone model and, if you have an account, the email you sign in with.

Getting started

How do I log a transaction?

Open Log and type it like a text: - coffee 5.25 for spending or + salary 4.2k for income. Her Budget picks the category for you. Shortcuts make amounts faster: k adds three zeros, so 12k is 12,000. Tap a quick-add chip for things you log often.

How does Settle up work?

Type emma -24 dinner when you owe Emma, or emma +24 dinner when she owes you. When it's settled, tap Paid and it's added to your transactions.

Can I share a month with someone?

Yes. In Activity, tap Download on any month to save it as a picture you can send on WhatsApp or anywhere else.

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Skip after the intro and use everything on your phone. Create a free account whenever you like, and the entries on your phone move into it, so they're backed up and available on your other devices.

Your account

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, type your email, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you.

I didn't get the confirmation or reset email

Check your spam or promotions folder, and make sure you typed the right address. If it still isn't there after 10 minutes, email us.

I'm moving to a new phone

Install Her Budget on the new phone and sign in with the same account. Everything comes back, Premium included. If you've been using the app without an account, create one on your old phone first, so your entries move with you.

Premium

What does Premium include?

  • Unlimited transactions and settle-ups (the free plan includes 20 of each per month).

  • Unlimited categories, auto-assign rules, amount shortcuts and quick-add chips (5 of each on the free plan).

  • Your full history (the free plan shows the last 3 months; older entries stay saved).

Prices are shown in the app in your local currency, monthly or yearly.

Can I subscribe without an account?

Yes. Afterwards, the app asks you to create a free account. That links Premium to you, so it stays with you if you change or reset your phone.

I paid but Premium isn't showing

Go to Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. If you have a Her Budget account, sign in first.

How do I cancel?

  • iPhone: open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Her Budget → Cancel Subscription.

  • Android: open Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Her Budget → Cancel.

You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds are requested from them: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google through Google Play refunds.

Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Her Budget account (developer: [your developer name as shown on the App Store and Google Play]).

In the app

  1. Open Her Budget and go to Settings.

  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email herappsinc@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We'll delete it within 7 days and email you to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account, email address, and all your transactions, settle-ups, categories, rules and settings.

  • Removed from backups within 30 days: encrypted backup copies.

  • Kept: payment records that Apple, Google and RevenueCat must keep for tax and accounting.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel Premium. Cancel it in your subscriptions as shown above.

Report a problem

Email us with what happened, what you expected, your phone model and the app version (shown in your phone's app settings). A screenshot helps a lot.
