Her Budget Privacy Policy

Effective date: [date you publish this page]

Her Budget is a money diary app made by [your legal name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have. If anything is unclear, email us at herappsinc@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your information.

  • You can use Her Budget without an account. Your entries then stay on your phone and we can't see them.

  • If you create an account, your entries are stored securely so you can use them on any device you sign in on.

  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, right in the app.

What we collect

If you use Her Budget without an account

Everything you enter (transactions, settle-ups, categories and settings) is saved only on your phone. It isn't sent to us. If you delete the app, that information is deleted with it.

In the iPhone and Android app, our subscription provider RevenueCat gives your phone a random anonymous ID so you can subscribe without an account. This ID isn't linked to your name or email.

If you create an account

  • Account details: your email address and an account ID. If you sign up with email, your password is stored in a scrambled (hashed) form that no one can read, us included.

  • Sign in with Apple or Google: we receive the email address and basic profile details (such as your name) that Apple or Google share with us. With Apple you can choose "Hide My Email", and we then receive a private relay address instead.

  • What you enter in the app: transactions (amount, description, category and date), settle-ups (the names you type for friends, amounts, notes and whether they're paid), your categories, auto-assign rules, amount shortcuts, quick-add chips and currency.

Names you type in settle-ups are only used to show your own list back to you. Please don't enter more about other people than you need.

If you subscribe to Premium

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or bank details. We receive your subscription status: which plan you chose, when it renews or ends, and which store it came from. RevenueCat also receives your purchase history and basic device information, such as the app version and operating system, so it can confirm your subscription.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, or bank account logins, and we don't use advertising or analytics tools.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: save your entries, keep them in sync across your devices and sign you in.

  • To apply the free plan limits and unlock Premium when you subscribe.

  • To send account emails you ask for, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.

  • To answer you when you contact support.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't build profiles about you.

Who helps us run Her Budget

We use these service providers. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: stores accounts and entries, handles sign-in and sends account emails. Privacy policy.

  • RevenueCat: keeps track of subscriptions across Apple and Google. Privacy policy.

  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple, Google sign-in, and in-app payments, under their own privacy policies.

  • Google (Gmail): delivers account emails on Supabase's behalf.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We would only disclose it if the law requires us to, for example in response to a valid court order.

Where your information is stored

Account data is stored on Supabase servers in [the region you picked, e.g. Singapore]. RevenueCat stores subscription records in the United States. Our providers use industry-standard safeguards when information moves between countries.

How long we keep it

  • Without an account: on your phone until you delete the app or its data.

  • With an account: until you delete your account. When you do, your account and all of your entries are deleted from our database right away. Copies in encrypted backups are removed automatically within 30 days.

  • Purchase records: Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep records of payments as required for tax and accounting.

Your choices and rights

  • See or edit your information: everything you've entered is in the app, and you can change it there. Email us if you'd like a copy.

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account. You can also email us from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete it within 7 days.

  • Cancel Premium: deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

  • Stop using the app: you can stop at any time. Deleting the app removes anything saved only on your phone.

Depending on where you live, for example the European Union, the United Kingdom, California or Indonesia, you may have further rights, such as to object to or restrict how your information is used, or to complain to your data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can only read its own entries, and passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you.

Children

Her Budget isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app or by email before they take effect.
