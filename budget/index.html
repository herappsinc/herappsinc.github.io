Her Budget

A money diary you fill in like a chat. Type "- coffee 5.25" or "+ salary 4.2k" and Her Budget logs it, sorts it into a category and adds it to your month.

  • Log spending and income in one line
  • Automatic categories, plus your own keyword rules
  • Settle up: track who owes who, and tap Paid when it's done
  • A monthly overview by category, and every transaction by date
  • Download any month as an image and share it
  • Use it without an account, or sign in with email, Apple or Google to back up your entries

Free to download, with an optional Premium subscription for unlimited logging and your full history.

Available on the App Store for iPhone.
